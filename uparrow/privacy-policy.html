Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Jerry PM Dev ("we", "us", or "our") built the UpArrow app as a free game supported by ads and optional in-app purchases. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

UpArrow does not directly collect any personal information from users. We do not require account registration, login, or any form of personal data input.

However, our app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google AdMob - We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data such as device identifiers, IP address, and usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing - Coin packs and Remove Ads are bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment and your payment details; we only receive confirmation of which item was purchased so the app can deliver it.

Your Ad Choices

Where required by law (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, using Google's consent tools. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Watching ads for rewards (such as free coins) is always optional, and buying Remove Ads turns off the ads shown between levels.

Data Collected by Third Parties

Google AdMob may collect the following types of data:

  • Device information (device model, operating system version)
  • Advertising ID
  • IP address
  • App usage data and interaction with ads

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with their privacy policy. We do not have access to or control over this data.

Game Data

UpArrow stores your game data (levels cleared, stars, coins, skins and settings) locally on your device using Android DataStore. This data:

  • Is stored only on your device
  • Is not transmitted to any server
  • Is not shared with any third party
  • Can be deleted with Settings → Reset progress, by clearing the app data, or by uninstalling the app (a Remove Ads purchase stays linked to your Google Play account)

Children's Privacy

UpArrow is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
